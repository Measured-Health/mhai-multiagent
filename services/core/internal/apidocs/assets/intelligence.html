Intelligence API (NATS)

The intelligence service has no HTTP API. Its operations are reached over NATS, never over HTTP, and only core (and, for the operations marked BFF direct, the clinical BFF after a grant from core) may call them. Every message carries a core-signed request token; a worker checks it before it reads the body.

This page is generated from the .proto files in proto/ and core's operations table. Subjects, users and streams are described in services/nats/README.md.

How a call is checked

Before any work, a worker checks, in order: sizes, the token's shape and signature, its claims, its binding to the subject and body, the reply subject, replay, the schema, the scope (every hospital and patient id in the body matches the token) and finally the permission: the operation's roles above must include one of the caller's roles, or svc:core for a service call. A failed check does no work and answers with one of the error codes below. See spec section 7.3 (docs/specs/2026-10-28-intelligence-boundary-design.md).

Error codes

A worker's Error message holds one of these codes and a retry hint, and no free text.

CodeMeaning
unauthorizedA verification check failed (size, token, claims, binding, reply subject or replay).
forbiddenThe role check failed: the operation's entry in the operations table allows none of the caller's roles.
invalid_requestThe schema or scope check failed.
not_foundThe row is not visible under the caller's hospital.
deadline_exceededThe call was received after its token expired.
overloadedThe worker is at its concurrency limit.
unavailableA model or the database is not ready.
cancelledThe job was cancelled.
internalAnything else.
