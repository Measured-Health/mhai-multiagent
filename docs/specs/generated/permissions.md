# Permission table

Generated by `services/core/cmd/permgen` from the core service's routes. Do not edit by hand;
run `go run ./cmd/permgen` in `services/core` after changing a route.

- **Access:** `public` needs no token; `signed in` is any account whose session meets its two-step policy;
  `enrolment` also accepts a session limited to setting up two-step.
- **Roles:** any one of them. Roles combine on clinical accounts.
- **Audience:** `platform` needs `X-MHAI-Audience: platform` and a platform account (else 403 `not_platform`);
  `clinical` refuses platform accounts (403 `forbidden`); `both` (the account's own routes) is either account on its own audience.
- **Write:** auditors get 403 `forbidden`; they are also refused every method but GET, HEAD and OPTIONS outside the `both` routes.
- Another hospital's resource is 404 `not_found`, never 403.

## Operations

The calls core may make to an intelligence worker over NATS, from `internal/authz/operations.go`.
The same table is published as `services/intelligence/intelligence/boundary/operations.json`.

- **Roles:** any one of them; `-` means no user may call it.
- **svc:core:** core may call it with no user. **Hospital:** the body is scoped to the caller's hospital.
- **BFF direct:** the BFF may call it after a grant from core. **Test only:** refused in production.
